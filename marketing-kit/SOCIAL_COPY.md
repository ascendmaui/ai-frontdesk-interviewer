## Do not add

- Star ratings, follower counts, “trusted by” lines, or quotes.
- Percentages of calls answered, revenue recovered, or time saved.
- “Replaces your receptionist.”
- A claim that the customer agent runs on Grok. This repo names Grok Voice for hiring interviews only.
- Discount codes or “spots left” urgency. Sales training forbids fake scarcity.
