## About 100 words

Hearthline builds AI Front Desk, AI employees for local businesses. AI Receptionist is $499 to set up and $299 a month: 24/7 voice, missed-call text-back, lead qualification, booking, and summaries. AI Front Office, the flagship, is $999 to set up and $799 a month and adds website chat, text and email follow-up, CRM and pipeline, a review engine, and monthly optimization. AI Growth Partner is $2,500 to set up and $1,900 a month and adds an outbound sales agent, AI search/ChatGPT ads, Meta ads, landing pages and creative, and a strategist. Demo requests are matched to a closer by phone area code.

## Boilerplate

Hearthline’s AI Front Desk is sold to local, appointment- and estimate-driven businesses. Seats documented in this repository include HVAC, plumbing, electrical, med spa, dental, auto body, auto service, law, roofing, landscaping, cleaning, remodeling, and pest control. Package pitches used by the company are: “Every call answered, every lead captured” (Receptionist); “Your whole front desk, automated — phones, chat, follow-up, and reviews as one system” (Front Office); and “The full growth machine — front office plus outbound, ads, and content, managed for you” (Growth Partner). Those lines are positioning, not measured performance. The product is not described as a substitute for a human receptionist, and it is not described as giving medical, legal, or financial advice.
