## FAQ

**What is AI Front Desk?**
Hearthline’s product for local businesses. The tagline is “AI employees for local businesses.”

**What does AI Receptionist include?**
A 24/7 AI voice agent, missed-call text-back, lead qualification, appointment booking, and call summaries. Setup is $499 and the monthly price is $299.

**What is the flagship?**
AI Front Office. Setup $999, then $799 a month. It includes the Receptionist, plus a website chat agent, automated text and email follow-up, CRM and pipeline, a review engine, and monthly optimization.

**What is AI Growth Partner?**
Setup $2,500, then $1,900 a month. It includes Front Office, plus an AI outbound sales agent, AI search/ChatGPT ads, Meta ads, landing pages and creative, and a dedicated strategist.

**Does this replace a receptionist?**
The sales curriculum says the AI can complement staff rather than replace them, and that some conversations should stay human, with a handoff. Nights, weekends, overflow, missed calls, and follow-up are the gaps it is meant to cover.

**Will it give medical, legal, or financial advice?**
No. Conduct rules say never to provide medical, legal, financial, or other regulated professional advice on behalf of a client. Dental training says the AI does not diagnose. Law-firm training says it must not give legal advice.

**Do you guarantee results?**
This repository does not publish a conversion rate, revenue result, uptime figure, or performance guarantee. Sales training says not to promise those unless an approved offer explicitly supports them.

**How do I get a demo?**
Submit the demo form. Requests are routed by the phone number’s area code to a closer who covers that code, when one is assigned.

**Are there other services?**
Yes. The product notes also list AI Voice Receptionist, AI Sales Agent, Support Assistant, Website Chat Agent, websites and landing pages, follow-up workflows, CRM setup, review engine, Meta ads, and agent installs. Anything outside the standard package has to be confirmed before it is promised.
